## Rating guidance

- 0 — Unseen: not studied.
- 1 — Familiar: recognize terminology and can follow a lesson.
- 2 — Working: explain and modify a known example.
- 3 — Independent: build, test, and debug without step-by-step guidance.
- 4 — Engineering judgment: select approaches, reason about trade-offs, secure, measure, and explain operational failure modes.

Only increase a rating when you can link practical evidence.
