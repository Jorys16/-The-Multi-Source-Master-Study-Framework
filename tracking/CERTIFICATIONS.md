## Readiness checklist for any exam

- [ ] Read the latest official exam guide.
- [ ] Map each exam domain to a course or project artifact.
- [ ] Complete relevant hands-on labs.
- [ ] Take an official or reputable practice assessment.
- [ ] Record weak areas and study them.
- [ ] Verify exam availability, version, language, delivery options, and fee.
- [ ] Schedule only when practical work supports the knowledge.
