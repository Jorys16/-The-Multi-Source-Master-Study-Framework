## Sprint review

At each milestone:
- Demonstrate the user journey.
- Show acceptance criteria and test evidence.
- Explain design decisions and trade-offs.
- Review security and authorization boundaries.
- Update unresolved risks.
- Decide what is explicitly out of scope.
