## Review method

- Same day: explain the concept from memory.
- +1 day: redraw the diagram and answer three retrieval questions.
- +3 days: solve a new example.
- +7 days: apply it to the capstone.
- +14 days: teach it back and compare with an adjacent concept.
- +30 days: include it in a cumulative exercise.

When a review fails, record the missing concept and revisit only the relevant explanation or example. Do not restart an entire course unnecessarily.
